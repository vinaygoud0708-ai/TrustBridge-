Executive Summary
High-level overview of the TrustBridge platform

TrustBridge is a next-generation transparent charity platform designed to solve the most critical problem in the non-profit world — the lack of trust and accountability between donors and charitable organizations.

Today, millions of well-intentioned donors hesitate to contribute because they cannot verify where their money goes. Simultaneously, hundreds of legitimate small NGOs struggle to gain credibility without trusted infrastructure. TrustBridge bridges this gap.

Our Solution
A centralized digital platform built on transparency
  • Verifies charities through a multi-tier process before allowing any fundraising activity
  • Collects donations through secure, PCI-DSS compliant multi-channel payment gateways
  • Tracks every single donation with a unique transaction ID and public ledger entry
  • Displays real-time fundraising progress publicly visible to anyone on the internet
  • Requires mandatory proof of fund usage before any withdrawal is processed
  • Provides comprehensive donor dashboards with impact reports and tax receipts
  • Builds trust through a dynamic trust scoring system and public audit trail
  • Offers an escrow-based fund release system for maximum donor protection

Step 1 — Charity Registration

  • NGO submits registration form
  • Uploads government registration certificate
  • Provides Tax ID / NTN / PAN details
  • Submits bank account for verification
  • Uploads founder/director identity documents
  • Admin reviews and verifies all documents
  • ✅ Once approved → Verified Badge assigned

Step 2 — Campaign Creation

  • Charity creates campaign with step-by-step wizard
  • Sets funding goal amount and deadline
  • Provides detailed budget breakdown plan
  • Uploads photos, videos, beneficiary data
  • Selects category and geo-location
  • ✅ Admin approves before campaign goes live

Step 3 — Donor Contribution

  • Donor registers or contributes as guest
  • Browses campaigns, filters by trust score
  • Selects donation amount and currency
  • Pays via secure gateway (card, wallet, bank)
  • Receives instant email receipt with unique TX ID
  • ✅ Donation recorded + visible in real-time counter

Step 4 — Escrow & Fund Holding

  • Funds held in TrustBridge platform escrow wallet
  • Charity cannot access funds immediately
  • Charity submits phased withdrawal request
  • Admin reviews request and supporting documents
  • ✅ Funds released in phases after approval

Step 5 — Fund Usage Reporting

  • Charity must upload proof before next withdrawal
  • Required evidence: Bills, invoices, bank statements
  • Photos and videos of project progress
  • Detailed usage report with item breakdown
  • Beneficiary data and impact metrics
  • ✅ All proof publicly visible to anyone
  • ✅ Donors receive automatic notifications

Compliance & Legal Framework
Legal requirements and risk protection

Legal Compliance Requirements

  • Company registration (PVT LTD or equivalent)
  • Financial services regulations (per country)
  • Data Protection Laws (GDPR / PDPL)
  • AML — Anti-Money Laundering regulations
  • KYC — Know Your Customer requirements
  • Tax compliance for donation receipts
  • PCI-DSS payment security standard
  • Consumer protection regulations

Essential Legal Documents

  • Terms & Conditions
  • Privacy Policy
  • Cookie Policy
  • Donation Refund Policy
  • Charity Partnership Agreement
  • Data Processing Agreement
  • AML / CFT Policy
  • Whistleblower Policy

Complaint & Dispute System

Users can:

  • Report a charity for misuse of funds
  • File a formal complaint with evidence
  • Request a refund within policy window
  • Report suspicious activity anonymously
  • Escalate unresolved disputes to senior admin
StageTimeline
AcknowledgmentWithin 24 hours
InvestigationWithin 7 business days
ResolutionWithin 14 business days

Audit Logs

System automatically logs:

  • Every admin approval action (who, when, what)
  • Every fund withdrawal (amount, approver, timestamp)
  • Every document upload (file hash for authenticity)
  • Every account modification
  • Every login attempt (IP + device)
📁 All logs stored minimum 7 years for legal compliance
